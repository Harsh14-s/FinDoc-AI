=== PAGE 9 ===
PROPOSED WORKFLOW
HUMAN REVIEW REMAINS ESSENTIAL
How FinDocAI will work
1. Document upload: submit a PDF or scanned image.
2. Parsing and OCR: parse digital PDFs and use OCR when required.
3. Structuring and normalization: identify selected fields and tables; 
normalize dates, amounts, and formats into tables or JSON.
4. Validation and flagging: check fields, types, calculations, missing values, 
mismatches, and unusual transactions; record reasons.
5. Dashboard, review, and export: display results and status, allow 
authorized correction, and export reviewed data plus a processing 
summary.
Human review remains essential: a flagged 
transaction is not proof of fraud, and the 
system does not make autonomous credit or 
financial decisions.
